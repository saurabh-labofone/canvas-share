Scope flag · newspapers

The newspaper hypothesis stays untested.

Reason one

No access to the paper

We have neither the physical editions nor the e-paper archives from here. There is nothing to read, so there is nothing to count.
Reason two

It is a paid-media channel

Newspaper recruitment sections are paid ad placements. Testing that channel means buying ads, not scraping them.
The nearest online proxy — OLX classifieds — carries 4,893 "office assistant" ads from micro-businesses, so the offline segment exists. It stays a hypothesis, not a finding.

01

The ads are real

The genuine EA and PA roles match the data: reports, calendar, invoices, follow-ups, general admin.

Most employers first asked us to come to the office.
