The money — how clients pay us

Three ways to charge.

1Monthly retainer. A fixed fee every month for a set number of videos.e.g. €1,000 / ₹1,00,000 a month for 4 videos — steady, predictable income.
2Per project. A price per video, with a small cut going to the shared pool.e.g. €400 / ₹40,000 a video → €60 / ₹6,000 to the pool, the rest split by who did the work.
3Split equally. Everything the collective earns, divided three ways — no matter who did more.e.g. a €900 / ₹90,000 job → €300 / ₹30,000 each. The Sidemen way.
We pick what fits each client — and write it down before the first invoice.
Keeping it fair — inside the team

Help is tracked as credits.

—Every favour earns credits. When one of us works on another’s video, we log it. No cash changes hands.
—We settle up every 3 months. Credits balance out — a favour back, pool cash, or first dibs on the next paid job.
—Your own income stays 100% yours. We only track the help, never your channel money.
Example · the ledger

Daniel shoots Bobby’s video+2

So helping each other never turns into unfair, unpaid work.
The shared pool

One pot we all own.

—We each put in a small cut of client money — say 15–20% off the top of every job.
—It’s ours to invest in whatever grows us — gear, editing tools, ads, our own videos, even a course.
—Big spends need all three of us. Small stuff, anyone can spend and log it.
Anyone can walk away with 30 days’ notice — and your channels are always yours.
