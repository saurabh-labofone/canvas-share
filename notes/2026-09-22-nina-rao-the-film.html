0 / The goal

Read first
The one film that tells her story properly, and travels.There are many pieces about Nina Rao on the internet. None of them tells the story. She is not famous, and she likes the background.

Our job is to make the one film that tells it, and to make it travel. The film must go viral. It must also be authentic, organic and impactful: nothing staged, nothing inflated, every fact sourced.

The closest reference for how we tell it is Yes Theory. A live journey on the ground, her history under narration, a cold open, real stakes, a golden moment, and an outro with meaning.

1 / The subject, in a minute

Every fact sourced in the research bank

Nina Rao. Born about 1964. Air India father, childhood across Thailand, Nairobi and India. At nine she sang call-and-response with her grandfather's foot-pedal harmonium in a village in Bekal, and recorded him on a five-button cassette recorder. She still has the tape and the handwritten lyrics.

New York, 1986. Positions clerk on a Japanese bank's trading floor, $14,700 a year, promoted to assistant trader. Quit in 1991 with no plan. Ran African and Indian safaris through the 1990s, founded Rare Earth Explorations in 1995, and felt "a tourist's guilt" that safaris did not help the tiger. Gave $125 each to two forest guards who caught poachers. That became Saving Wild Tigers: volunteer-run, no salary ever, funded from her own pocket since 2000.

1996. Walked into a Krishna Das kirtan in the Catskills expecting "a failed Indian musician". Stayed three hours at the front. Built his first web page on her safari domain, made him a business card that said "Have harmonium, will travel", and has been his manager since 2002.

1998. Met Siddhi Ma at Kainchi Dham, the woman who refused every photograph. Served and translated for her for 19 years. Sings herself now: the Hanuman Chalisa melody with 501K views, three albums, Bhumi Devi released 4 September 2026. Her Sacred Music for Sacred Forests concerts fund field scientists and forest guards.

Her vibe. Artificial intelligence against our ancient intelligence. Meeting people and bringing them together. Nature and the Name, her two refuges.

Fact guards for the edit
  • Never say where she was born. Never state her father's death year. Both unresolved.
  • "Wall Street trader" is defensible. "Banker" is safer.
  • The Hanuman Chalisa is about 400 years old. Never "ancient", never "1,000-year-old".
  • Krishna Das is Grammy-nominated, not a winner.
  • Siddhi Ma's face is never drawn and never shown. None exists by her wish.
  • "Saves tigers" means she pays for the people who protect them. Never "fights poachers".

2 / The conversation so far

Two calls, 3 and 18 September

Nina said yes. Her words, in short: "Let's document everything. Let's meet, and let's capture everything we have of my entire life." The story starts with the safari and why we need to save the world. She has a lot to say on artificial intelligence against our ancient intelligence, and on meeting people and bringing them together. That is her vibe. We put it all together.

Both calls were with Nina and her sister Sarina. No rush from her side. Every cut is hers to approve. She asked for one thing: an honourable mention of the people on the ground she has supported for 30 years.

3 / The film we are making

Format and spine

Working title lane. "How can a Wall Street trader save tigers with a 400-year-old prayer?" Exact wording open. Her name stays out of the title.

Narration-led. Saurabh narrates and drives, and carries at least two thirds of the words. Nina lands beats in one to three lines, in her own words. Two tracks: the live October journey, and her history under narration.

Her documents on camera. The 1997 trip log, the business card, the cassette, the charity's 2001 homepage. Real paper, real screens.

The spine is the trip. Corbett (tigers, money) → the road (the bank, why she left) → Kainchi (the guru, the 5:30 knock) → Rishikesh (the Catskills room opens the childhood scene, the long sit-down) → beside KD backstage → the concert on 19 October as the payoff.

Animation only for the past the camera cannot reach, drawn simpler the further back it goes. Miracles are told on camera, never drawn. About five minutes across eight sequences.

Runtime 20 to 25 minutes, as blessed by Nina. The film must stand on Rishikesh alone. Corbett and Kainchi are upside.

5 / What we owe her

The commitment, as written in the offer she read
  • The finished film, free. This is seva. No fee, no strings.
  • Every cut approved by Nina. She holds the last word on anything about Siddhi Ma.
  • Short clips for @ninaraochant and Saving Wild Tigers, theirs to use forever.
  • Every call-to-action points to donations: Centre for Wildlife Studies and Tiger Watch. Never to us.
  • Her mentors get their honourable mention: Valmik Thapar (died 31 May 2025), Ullas and Krithi Karanth, Go Dharmic's Sundarbans work.
  • No rush. She removed speed from the brief. Quality over speed.
  • Questions and locations shared with her before the shoot.
  • Nothing invented. No crisis, no deadline, no tiger sighting we did not get.
